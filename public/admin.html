Independent desk availability

Close new order intake while leaving Exchange and Bank open to finish queued trades. Existing orders stay in place unless you explicitly reject them.

Loading desks…

These gates are enforced by the database, not just the buttons.
Safe close sequence: close order intake → keep Pit / Exchange / Bank open to clear the queue → close the event → finalize only when all pending orders are resolved. Reject open orders is a separate bulk action and cannot be undone as a group.
Reset restores the locked starting baseline: official team/broker assignments, company base prices and all uploaded IPO allotments. IPO allotment prices and quantities are preserved, and saved IPO listing prices are retained for the next Start Event. Winner rule: highest Net Worth at close — Net Worth = liquid cash + (shares × closing price). The final closing cash criterion is ₹1,00,000, excluding cash from realized profits.
