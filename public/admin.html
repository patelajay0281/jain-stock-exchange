Event lifecycle

Use these controls in the normal order: Start → Pause/Resume → Close → Finalize.

Finalization closes the event and performs the configured outstanding-loan repayment sweep above the ₹20,000 minimum cash buffer. The final topper is selected only from teams that used at least ₹19,80,000 of their ₹20,00,000 own allocation at peak, fully repaid loan principal + interest, maintained at least ₹20,000 final cash, and had no settled short sale. Among eligible teams, highest realized profit wins; if none has realized profit, least loss wins. Use only after all settlement activity is complete.
