Institutional Investors

When a participant cannot find a counterparty, the institution trades with it: the participant BUYS at 2% above the prevailing market price and SELLS at 2% below. The participant pays the normal brokerage, earned by its broker, and the trade goes through the Pit Manager, Exchange and Bank like any order.
