This dashboard can verify that the application’s authenticated read APIs respond and can show the live order-pipeline aggregates returned by the Insights API. It cannot read Cloudflare request logs, historical 1101/1102 exceptions, D1/Neon query contention, exact queue age, or account quota from the browser. Those require a protected server-side telemetry integration. Capacity targets above are not presented as achieved performance.
